Third Grade English

Homework this week (REVISED Mon 11/16) — assigned Fri 11/13, due Fri 11/20

  • Read in English 15–30 minutes each day. No Scholastic News magazine or quiz this week (optional: finish older issues or read online).
  • Practice the skills on the attached phonics review pages.
  • Read the Fluency Passages "Jazz the Doll" and "Duck in a Truck" aloud 2–3 times to an adult, highlight the ck words and f/l/s/z double-letter words, then get a parent signature.
  • Correction: Friday's email had last week's homework scan attached by mistake — the list above is the corrected one. (Ignore "Paddling Into History" / "The Gash.")
  • Spelling test Friday, Nov 20. Heart words: great and warm. The rest is applying the phonics rules — there's no word list, so practice with any words that follow them:
    ① c / k / ck for the /k/ sound — c before a, o, u (cat, cot, cup); k before e, i, y (kid, sky); ck after a short vowel at the end of a one-syllable word (duck, neck).
    ② FLOSS rule — double f, l, s, z after a short vowel at the end of a one-syllable word (stuff, hill, mess, buzz).
    ③ Digraphs — only sh, ch, th, wh on the test (ship, chin, thumb, whale).
  • In reading they're learning the elements of plot (beginning, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution) with a "plot mountain" — ask Cooper what the problem in a story is and how it gets solved.
  • Questions? Reach her at lisa.mchugh@minnetonkaschools.org.
